Dev log: building Skull World in a day

November 1, 2026

Skull World is an upcoming game for Steam, inspired by my admiration for catsoup's Blender work (catsoup is a member of TeamSESH). It's a Windows desktop game I'm making in Godot 4. Today I got two maps playable, Town and a foggy Silent Hill-style Woods, both modeled in Blender and loaded at runtime from a launcher hub.

Most of the day went into Survival, a round-based mode against skeletons: points, a Skeleton Vault for random weapons, perk stands run by skeleton NPCs, drops, per-weapon reloads, melee, aim down sights and a third-person camera. Skeletons climb out of manholes, coffins and the ground, and the houses now have doors you can close.
