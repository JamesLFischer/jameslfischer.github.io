Skull World · Game dev · 2026

A night town,
a foggy woods
and skeletons.

Skull World is an upcoming game for Steam, inspired by my admiration for catsoup's Blender work (catsoup is a member of TeamSESH). It is a Windows desktop game I am building in Godot 4. A launcher hub opens two environments, Town and Woods. Explore lets you walk each map. Survival is a round-based mode against skeletons. Maps are modeled in Blender and loaded at runtime.
