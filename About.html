04 / About me

I’ve always liked figuring out how things work — and then figuring out how to make them work better.

I grew up around the Seattle area and first got interested in computing through gaming. That curiosity eventually became hands-on projects: online communities, digital products, websites, software coursework, and now real-world infrastructure work.

What I enjoy most is the intersection between technical depth and practical outcomes. I’m comfortable moving from a command line or codebase to a business process, a customer problem, or a team conversation.
