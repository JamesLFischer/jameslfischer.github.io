Render_04 - PushingUpDaisies

November 23, 2026

PushingUpDaisies is Render 04 from the VHS low-poly pipeline: a 26.0 second, 720×480 clip, built locally on an 8 GB GPU.

The picture is a night village. Wooden houses, a cross, a skeletal hand, a CRT, and a deer. The camera stays handheld for the full 26 seconds.

Master

26.0 s, 24 fps, 624 frames. 720×480 progressive H.264, with AAC at 44.1 kHz.

Plates

Plates and keyframes are local ComfyUI on the 8 GB GPU. RealVisXL V5, with Union ControlNet on canny guides.

3D

The 3D elements are 624 frames in Blender Cycles, with AgX color management. Rendered at 1080×810, 16-bit RGBA, 40–48 spp, then OpenImageDenoise.

Motion

Three pose-continuous image-to-video segments. Each frame is matted with ISNet, then fringe cleanup and a 3-frame temporal edge blend.

Compositor

The Python compositor is OpenCV, NumPy, SciPy, and Pillow. One C2 cubic-spline camera curve covers the 26 seconds, handheld. Ground-plane tracking uses the legs only. Hampel outlier cleanup and a Gaussian smooth (σ=5) hold vertical drift under 0.6 px.

The same pass runs the animated VHS signal: chroma bleed, grain, head-switch noise, and layer-protected tracking and dropouts. The frames are piped into FFmpeg.

Audio

The PushingUpDaisies sample is synced, with CRT-speaker EQ and an ambient tape bed. The master is -17.0 LUFS. A logarithmic fade runs from 24.2 s to 26.0 s. Premiere Pro applies the ownership watermark and writes the export.

Footage and audio sample: @grabmypepsi.
