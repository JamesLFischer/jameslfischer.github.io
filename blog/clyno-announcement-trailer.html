Announcing clyno: a tutor for each of your classes

November 26, 2026

clyno is a web app that gives each of your classes its own AI tutor, then tests you on what you're learning. Here is the 44-second announcement trailer.

What it does

Connect your school apps (Canvas, Moodle, Blackboard, Brightspace, Schoology and more) and clyno creates a subject for each class, pulling in everything it can as context for that subject's tutor. Upload your own notes, slides and PDFs, and the tutor can use them as context to teach and quiz you. The tutor quizzes you on your class material and explains every miss by quoting your own notes. Spaced review with the FSRS algorithm brings weak topics back before you forget them, and timed practice exams are built around them. It tracks your grade against your passing line and how ready you are for each exam, with push reminders before due dates.

How it's built

Next.js 15, React 19, TypeScript and Tailwind CSS 4 on Vercel. Supabase for Auth and Postgres, with row-level security and pgvector for retrieval over your course material. LMS sync, the ingest pipeline and FSRS scheduling (ts-fsrs) are written in the app. Stripe for payments, Venice for inference, or students can bring their own key. It's an installable PWA with web push.

The trailer

The trailer is built in Remotion from clyno's own screens, with an original track made for it.
